## About

I am a medical physicist in training from Bangladesh, working at the intersection of **radiation oncology, medical imaging, and responsible AI**. My goal is to develop research and open-source tools that help make cancer care more equitable and more reliable across populations, especially in South Asian settings.

My current focus is on whether radiomics and dosiomics models generalize beyond the populations in which they are developed—and how explainability, domain-shift analysis, and careful validation can support safer translation into clinical research.

## Research focus

- **Medical physics & radiation oncology** — quantitative methods for improving cancer treatment research
- **Radiomics & dosiomics** — extracting reproducible imaging and dose-based biomarkers
- **Explainable AI** — making model behavior easier to inspect and communicate
- **Cross-population generalizability** — evaluating performance under demographic and domain shift
- **Open science for global health** — transparent, reproducible workflows for resource-aware research

### [LUNG-RP-XAI](https://github.com/iamSHShUvo/LUNG-RP-XAI)

**Cross-Population Generalizability of Lung Cancer Radiomics and Dosiomics: Implications for AI Deployment in a South Asian Cohort**

A research project studying the transportability of lung cancer radiomics and dosiomics models, with an emphasis on radiation pneumonitis, explainability, and responsible deployment. The project is documented with a [Zenodo preprint](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20927951) and released under the MIT license.
