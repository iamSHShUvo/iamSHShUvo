## Research profile

I am a **Medical Physics & Biomedical Engineering graduate** focused on reliable AI for cancer research. My work sits at the intersection of **medical imaging, radiomics, dosiomics, radiation therapy, explainable AI, and cancer bioinformatics**.

My central research question is: **can a model trained on one population, scanner, or institution be trusted when applied to another?** I build reproducible clinical-data pipelines and evaluate model generalizability, with particular interest in underrepresented South Asian cohorts.

I am preparing for graduate research at the intersection of **clinical medical physics, quantitative imaging, trustworthy machine learning, and equitable cancer care**.

## Research experience

### Research Assistant | Square Hospitals Ltd. & Ahsania Mission Cancer and General Hospital
**Jan 2025 – Apr 2026 · Dhaka, Bangladesh**

- Conducted a lung-cancer analytical study using clinical imaging data from two major oncology centres.
- Developed a forensic DICOM-processing pipeline that recovered **92.5% of corrupted local cases**.
- Extracted **107 radiomic and dosiomic features across 597 patients from three institutions**.
- Quantified cross-population generalizability and investigated underrepresentation of South Asian cohorts in radiomics and dosiomics validation.
- Developed machine-learning classification models and applied SHAP-based explainability.
- Worked alongside medical physicists on CT simulation, treatment-plan review, and clinical QA, including IMRT, VMAT, and DIBH-3DCRT workflows.
- Open-sourced the research codebase under the MIT license: [LUNG-RP-XAI](https://github.com/iamSHShUvo/LUNG-RP-XAI).

### Research Intern | Center for Bio-Health Research Bangladesh
**Oct 2025 – Apr 2026 · Competitively selected · Remote**

- Conducted a systematic review of **ANKRD22 across 15+ cancer types**, cross-verifying 32 primary studies against PubMed and DOI records.
- Mapped three oncogenic axes: NuSAP1/Wnt, E2F1, and glycolytic reprogramming, including the gene’s protective role in immune cells.
- Identified the absence of a TCGA-wide pan-cancer analysis as a central research gap and designed a follow-up workflow covering expression, survival, immune infiltration, methylation, and pathway enrichment.
